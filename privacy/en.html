About the app

This policy explains how Tilt Puzzle handles information. The app has no account registration or login, and Madsun does not operate an online game-save service. Gameplay does not ask you to submit your name, email address, contacts, photographs or microphone recordings. Bundled third-party services are described separately below.

Game progress, settings and backups

The app stores game progress, unlocked stages, best turn counts and language preferences locally. Some progress and settings also have a local Android Preferences backup. App and device language preferences help select the displayed language; the app does not perform its own GPS or IP lookup for this purpose.

The app does not upload these game saves to a Madsun server. Android backup, restoration or device transfer may include app data depending on the operating system, device and your settings. Local storage does not guarantee that data stays on one device.

Game progress and settings have no fixed expiry. They remain until cleared, replaced or removed through app or Android data management. Android app-data controls can clear local data; restoration from a backup may bring data back. Restarting a puzzle does not delete all saved progress or settings. Local deletion does not request deletion of information held by Google or remove every backup.

Advertising and Google services

In the current Android 1.3.0 configuration, advertising requests and User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent requests are blocked, including test advertising requests. The app includes Google Mobile Ads and UMP libraries. The Android advertising ID (AD_ID) permission and the AdServices ad ID, attribution and topics permissions are removed from this build. Removing permissions or blocking advertising requests does not establish that all bundled components process no information.

Google describes its advertising SDK as potentially processing network-derived approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device-related identifiers. Processing depends on the service, configuration, platform and settings. Google’s handling, recipients, retention and international processing are described in its Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Privacy Policy. This policy does not claim that bundled SDKs collect no data.

The app’s privacy menu provides access to this policy and privacy-choice actions. In the current blocked configuration, these actions report availability without opening a UMP consent form or requesting an ad. A status message is not consent to personalized advertising.

Age groups and availability

Tilt Puzzle is intended for teenagers and adults in the 13–15, 16–17 and 18+ age groups. It is not designed for children under 13. Selecting these target groups does not automatically prevent installation by a person under 13. The app currently does not ask for a date of birth or provide an age screen. Local privacy and consent requirements may still apply to teenagers.

Madsun intends global distribution wherever Tilt Puzzle is made available through Google Play. Actual availability depends on the store and region.

Contact, requests and inquiry retention

You may email tjsrb424@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests concerning information held by Madsun, including requests to access, correct or delete it. Please describe your request without sending unnecessary sensitive information. The operator may ask for information reasonably needed to identify and handle the request. Available rights and the handling of a request depend on applicable law. The app creates no user accounts and therefore has no app-account deletion flow.

Sending email shares your address and message with Madsun and the email service used for the exchange. Madsun deletes the support and privacy inquiry emails it retains within 90 days after the inquiry is closed. If applicable law requires retention, Madsun retains only the information subject to that requirement for the legally required period and deletes it when that obligation ends. This period applies to inquiry emails retained by Madsun, not to Google SDK data or Android backups.
